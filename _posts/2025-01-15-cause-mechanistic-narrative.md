What does it mean to call something a *cause* in a scientific domain? In this article, with L. C. Moore and M. Krutsky, we argue for an ordinary-language and mechanistic reading of causal claims, and critique attempts to turn “causal” into a domain-independent mathematical label detached from scientific narrative and mechanism.

The larger concern is methodological: mathematics is indispensable for science, but formal machinery should clarify the inferential and mechanistic content of a domain rather than silently replace it.
