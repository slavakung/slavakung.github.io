## Recent work — running summary

**2026.** The current year extends the OORDSU theme in several directions: real-time control of nonlinear hyperbolic PDE models for water engineering; learning the global/local trade-off in optimization; iterative linear algebra across heterogeneous parallel backends; rare-event stochastic programming for power systems; and three-tier control architectures. Parallel work develops symplectic neural networks for generalized Hamiltonians, decentralized derivative-free optimization, stochastic acceleration, sparse optimization, quantum algorithms, dataset distillation, and data-driven biomedical applications.

**2025.** Work ranges from stochastic interior-point methods and parametric semidefinite programming to dataset distillation, federated learning, autonomous-system control, scientific ML, solver-assisting machine learning, robust/Byzantine federated learning, and optimization under scientific structure. This period substantially expands the connection between optimization theory and deployment-oriented AI.

**2024.** Publications include variational quantum algorithm complexity, Riemannian derivative-free optimization, functional constrained stochastic optimization, bilevel methods, time-varying SDP, federated learning, and dataset distillation at CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS, and ICLR-associated work.
